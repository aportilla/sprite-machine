Sprite Machine Read Me

Draw an object from the front, the side and the top with pixel-art tools, and Sprite Machine turns the drawings into a blocky 3D model. Spin it around, save it for a game engine, or turn it into a sprite atlas: a sheet of the object seen from every angle.

QUICK START

Double-click Car on the desktop. Its window opens on the car's left side, and four small windows appear around it: the tools at the top left, the Color Palette under them, the Full Sprite View at the top right with the car's six faces in a row, and the 3D View under that. Draw on the car and the 3D View changes with every stroke. ⌘Z undoes. Check "rotate" at the top of the 3D View to spin the model. Click another face in the Full Sprite View to draw on it. ⌘S saves.

THE SIX FACES

A document is one object drawn from six directions: Front, Back, Left, Right, Top and Bottom. The line at the bottom of the document window names the face you are on. Draw each face as you would see it standing there. On the Left face the object's front points left, on the Right face it points right, and on the Top face it is at the top of the square.

The model is built as if carving a block: the Front face cuts away everything outside its outline, then the side face, then the Top. So the faces have to agree. A car 14 pixels long in the Left face is 14 pixels long in the Top face too. The faint picture behind the canvas is the opposite face, mirrored, and the strips along the edges show where the neighboring faces have pixels. Use them to line things up. You do not have to draw all six faces. An empty face is filled in from its opposite, and most objects need only the Front, one side and the Top.

Both sides of a step in one color make a smooth slope, like a windshield. Two colors keep a sharp edge.

DRAWING

Pick a tool in the Tools window or with its key: Selection (S), Pencil (B), Rectangle (R), Fill (G), Eraser (E) and Eyedropper (I). The bar under the menu bar shows the tool's settings, with the current color at its left. Click the color to change it, click a swatch in the Color Palette, or Option-click a pixel. Edit → Tile Size… changes the size of the faces, from 1 to 64 pixels.

LAYERS

A part that would get joined to the rest if drawn with it, such as wheels under a car, goes on its own layer. Layer → New Layer… (⌘K) adds one. Switch layers with the keys 1 to 8, the Layer menu's list, or the popup at the bottom of the document window. Check "single layer" in the 3D View to see only the layer you are editing.

SAVING

Documents are saved in your browser and live as icons on the desktop. File → Save (⌘S) saves; the first save asks you to confirm the name. A document that has never been saved is lost if you reload the page. File → Download saves the document to your disk as a .png that holds everything. Drag it onto the page on any computer to open it again.

Drag icons into folders, rename them with Return, and drag them to the Trash to delete them. Special → Empty Trash… is what removes them for good.

EXPORTING

File → Export 3D Model… saves a .glb file, which Unity, Godot, Unreal, Blender and most 3D tools open. File → Export Sprite Atlas… draws the model from a ring of angles and saves the pictures with a data file for 2D engines. View → 3D Sprite Atlas shows the ring before you export.

The full guide is at https://github.com/aportilla/sprite-machine
