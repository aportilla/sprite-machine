Sprite Machine turns low-resolution pixel-art face sprites (top / front / side / …) into a real, rotatable 3D object — the chunky look is baked into the geometry, not faked by a shader. One sheet, six tiles: a 3×2 atlas is carved into a voxel solid by multi-view visual-hull reconstruction — 1 pixel = 1 voxel = 1 cube — and smoothed with additive 45° wedges.
